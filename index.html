Featured project

Nueva Ecija AFK CCTV & Solar Installation Services

A real local-business page I managed on Facebook: building brand awareness in Nueva Ecija and turning educational content into quality inquiries and paying clients.

Chapter 1

The objective

Increase local brand awareness while generating quality inquiries through educational and promotional content.

Chapter 2

The strategy

Facebook marketing, SEO optimization, a 30-day content calendar, reels, and educational graphics. Content themes ran every day of the week: Educational Monday, Tips Tuesday, Behind-the-scenes Wednesday, Customer Story Thursday, Promotion Friday, FAQ Saturday and Community Sunday.

SEO plan: the project started with keyword research: a primary keyword (“Affordable CCTV Installation Nueva Ecija”), supporting keywords such as home security, solar installation and security cameras, then a meta title and meta description to protect the home with professional CCTV installation and trusted after-sales support.

Chapter 3

The content, live

Real posts for a real client: CCTV installation, solar installation works, how solar works, weatherproof design, and proof-of-install posts with real device photos. Content ideas included Why install CCTV, Benefits of solar energy, Before and after installation, customer testimonials and promo posts.

Results I delivered: using Facebook and organic targeting of the local market, I brought the page clients including a hospital, a university, a home, business stalls and joint-venture partners. These are real jobs, documented in the proof-of-work section below.

I don’t publish traffic or revenue figures for this page, only the real jobs and posts shown here.
